Community feedback received — reworking

We're reworking the site based on community feedback.
Reddit wasn't pleased, and we want to keep Reddit happy.

What's happening

We launched, Reddit reviewed, feedback was brutally honest (as expected), and now we're making it better. We're listening — that's the whole point of this project.

What we changed based on your feedback

  • Petition no longer requires Steam login — you can sign without logging in. Steam URL/ID is optional, used only to keep the signer list unique.
  • Kernel-level anti-cheat is not the main demand — wording updated. The focus is on Valve investing proportionally in anti-cheat and the game overall.
  • Community opinions section — a dedicated page where you can submit and vote on what you think Valve should change. Top-voted ideas shape the open letter.
  • "AI slop" comments — fair point. One person, limited dev experience, trying to build something useful quickly. No sensitive data handled. Fully open-source — contribute or audit anytime.
  • Support/tips — never forced, never pushed. Covers server costs, surplus reinvested into community tools. Stated transparently on the site.

What's coming back (improved)

Petition — sign without Steam login
Community Opinions — vote on what matters
Revenue Tracker — real Steam Market data
Media Wall — cheater clips and evidence
The Open Letter — delivered August 31, 2026
